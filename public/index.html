A LITTLE COURT SENSE

Find the T.
Find your rhythm.

Hit the front wall. Take turns returning the ball before its second bounce. Win a rally when your opponent misses, hits the tin, or sends it out.

CLICK THE COURT / CORNER PADPlace the ball, clearlyClick where you want the first floor bounce. Deep targets produce length; short targets produce drops. The dashed line previews your shot.
Z · C · E · Q · BBuild a rallyStraight length, crosscourt, drop, lob, boast. Straight shots default to the side wall, not the middle. Space swings the selected shot; press early to attempt a volley.
WASD / ARROWS · HOLD RMove with purposeAssisted footwork positions you beside the ball and recovers to the T. WASD overrides it. Hold R to recover manually, or turn assistance off in the clubhouse.
1 / 2 / 3 · V · ESCStay in control1 aims left; 2 uses the shot’s natural placement; 3 aims right. V switches between courtside and tactical cameras. Esc pauses.

Orange ball and trail = live ball. Orange floor ring = incoming bounce. White dashed line and lime target = your intended shot. First to 11, win by two. Arcade simplifications: no player interference or let/stroke decisions; serves use simplified placement.
